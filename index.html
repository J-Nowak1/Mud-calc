How it's figured

gal/ft = D² ÷ 24.51  ·  GPM = gal/ft × ft/min × mix ratio

D is the bit or reamer diameter in inches. 24.51 converts a one-foot cylinder from cubic inches to gallons (231 in³ per gallon). Mix ratios are typical starting points; adjust for returns, fluid properties and your mud engineer's program.

Mixing order

  1. Fill the tank with fresh water and add Soda Ash first to bring pH to about 8.5–9.5 and soften hard water.
  2. Add bentonite (BORE-GEL) slowly through the hopper so it doesn't fish-eye, and let it shear before adding polymer.
  3. Add polymers (QUIK-TROL GOLD, NO-SAG, POLY-BORE, EZ-MUD GOLD) slowly through the hopper.
  4. Add liquids (EZ-MUD, PENETROL, AQUA-CLEAR PFD) last.
  5. Check the mix with a Marsh funnel and adjust to your program.

About these numbers

Recipes pair Baroid IDP products by formation, using a Baroid dealer's HDD starting chart and the treatment ranges from Baroid's product data. Default rates sit near the low-middle of each range. Change any rate to match your mud program, water and returns. Job volume assumes no recycling: bore length × hole gal/ft × mix ratio from the Flow rate tab. With a recycler you'll mix less. Package sizes are common Baroid sizes; check yours with your supplier.
